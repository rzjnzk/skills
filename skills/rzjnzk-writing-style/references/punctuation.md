# Punctuation

## Dashes

- Em dash `—` (U+2014) with no space on either side: `word—word`.
- En dash `–` (U+2013) for ranges: `1990–1995`, `pages 4–9`.

## Brackets

- Avoid round brackets in paragraph prose; reserve them for Harvard-style citations and similar references.
- Round brackets are acceptable outside paragraph prose, such as in titles, and in Anki 'Word Meaning' fields to give context, e.g. 'talk (verb)' or 'talk (noun)'.
- Square brackets mark the writer's own additions within a quotation, and enclose [sic].

## Commas

- Always use the Oxford comma.

## Ellipses

- Mid-quotation: one space before and one space after the ellipsis.
- At the start of a quotation: no space between the opening quotation mark and the ellipsis, and one space after it.
- At the end of a quotation: one space before the ellipsis, and no space between it and the closing quotation mark.

Example: `'... some words ... other words ...'.`
